How do we balance the market by 2035?

Three levers the forecasts inform — raise supply, ease demand, or align the two.

This study quantifies the gap each lever must close: a sharp East-surplus / West-shortage divide by 2035. The longevity dividend (healthier, longer working lives) is the supply lever already in motion; retirement-age and participation policy are the fastest dials. See the balance report for country-level magnitudes.

Methodology — how the model works

Three diagrams: the 4-model ensemble engine, and how supply & demand are assembled into human-working-years.

Method in one paragraph

Each driver series is forecast on its full history by an ensemble of four simple models (linear trend, damped Holt, drift, naive) — damping and the naive anchor stop saturating rates from over-extrapolating. Population uses Eurostat's projection proj_23np, calibrated to observed 2024. Supply = healthy working-age people × expected working life; demand = (employed + vacancies) × required service. Monte-Carlo bands combine model disagreement, out-of-sample backtest error and demographic uncertainty.
