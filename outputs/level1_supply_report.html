How to read it. Supply = population (15–64) × healthy-life share (HLY/LE) × expected working life, in career person-years. It's a potential ceiling, net of health and typical working-life length, but before skills/frictions. The realization ratio (0.77) means ~24% of healthy working-age capacity is institutionally idle (later entry, early exit, non-participation) — the lever the West uses to offset shrinking populations. Population from Eurostat proj_23np (calibrated to 2024, D4); bands include out-of-sample model error (D5).

Caveats

• HLY is survey-based (GALI) with methodology breaks — the model can't beat a naive guess on it (see the model-trust report); treated as a bounded ratio.
• Forecasts assume the recent rise in working-life duration continues; the participation-plateau scenario (D6) flattens supply growth in the West.
• Supply feeds the Level 3 balance (supply − demand).
