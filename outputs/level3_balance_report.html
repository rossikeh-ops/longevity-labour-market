How to read it. Balance is in millions of career person-years. Positive = healthy working-age supply exceeds the labour the economy's jobs require (slack); negative = demand exceeds supply (shortage). Supply = health-adjusted working-age people × expected working life; demand = (employed + vacancies) × required service. Bands include out-of-sample model error (decision D5). The participation-plateau downside (D6) widens Western shortages further.

Caveats

• Supply is a potential ceiling — it abstracts from skills mismatch and frictions; read shortages/surpluses as structural capacity, not literal vacancies.
• Germany & France shortages depend on participation continuing to rise; under the plateau scenario they deepen.
• France vacancies carry Eurostat flag d; population from proj_23np calibrated to 2024 (D4).
• Drivers validated by rolling-origin backtest (see the model-trust report).
